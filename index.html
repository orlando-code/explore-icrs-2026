The ICRS 2026 Explorer

An interactive investigation of who came from where with what associated emissions, and how everyone fits together.

  • Map – speaker affiliations; search by name, institution, or talk keyword.
  • Emissions – estimated return-trip CO₂e to Auckland (economy flights where coming from overseas or further than 400 km from Auckland).
  • Network – co-authorship links between speakers; highlight people working in the same topics e.g. bleaching, OAE.

This is a simple tool and may contain errors. Please contact me (Orlando) at rt582@cam.ac.uk if you find any issues or have suggestions!

Regarding the emissions – from my hotel room in Auckland it would be incredibly hypocritical of me to say that we shouldn’t fly to conferences. They're invaluable opportunities to learn, collaborate, and share knowledge. But we must carefully consider the carbon cost of these connections and do everything we can to minimise our emissions and maximise the effectiveness of the collaborations gained!     Methods

    Data sources

    Speaker names, affiliations, and talk metadata come from the ICRS 2026 programme and delegate list emailed to all participants. Affiliations are geocoded to institutional locations (country-level coordinates are used where a precise campus location is unavailable).

    Travel emissions

    Return-trip CO₂e is estimated per attendee using the emissions.dev API. This uses a combination of emission factors and routing data to estimate the average emissions for a given journey between two points. For everyone further than 400 km from Auckland, the journey is assumed to be by plane. Given the deficit of trains in New Zealand, the non-plane overground emissions are assumed to be by car with an occupancy of 1.54 (the mean occupancy in urban areas).

    Network

    Nodes represent speakers or affiliations; edges connect people who share authorship on at least one ICRS talk. Node size reflects the number of talks on which that speaker or affiliation appears. Keyword search highlights nodes whose talks mention a term in the title or abstract, plus their co-authors on the graph.

    Selecting an individual shows public profile links found online via their name and affiliation. Note: these were generated automatically (see below) and may contain errors.

    Related talks

    Talk abstracts were analysed using a locally-hosted large language model (deepseek-r1:8b via the Ollama API). Abstracts were first clustered by keywords to reduce token number (decrease processing effort) following which the model was used to generate similarity scores between abstracts in the cluster. We display the top five most similar abstracts for each talk.

    Contact links

    Delegate contacts were gathered from public web pages (e.g. university staff pages, personal websites) via a combination of the Brave Search API and a custom search query using DuckDuckGo when I ran out of free credits.

    If for any reason you're uncomfortable with your information being displayed here, please contact me at rt582@cam.ac.uk or via the '?' on your author box: I'll remove it immediately! If you spot an error in a contact, or would like an alternative displayed for you, please let me know at the same address.

    Limitations

    • Geocoding is imperfect; some affiliations fall back to country centroids (arranged in a ring) or missed entirely. For 15% of delegates, no geographical affiliation could be found. This likely leads to underestimations of emissions. Furthermore, some delegates may not be geographically based at their named affiliation, or the institute may have multiple campuses.
    • A couple of hundred delegates could not be geocoded from their affiliation titles – these were excluded from the analysis. The exclusion of so many attendees definitely makes this a conservative estimate of total emissions.
    • Travel emissions are mean values – actual route travelled will affect the true value. Google Flights is a nice tool to see estimates of flight emissions!
    • Emissions do not include local transport, accommodation, or conference operations – although we were told that the conference centre, NZICC is EarthCheck Silver certified.
    • The delegate/author lists may not be completely up to date e.g. cancellations or changes to affiliations.
    • The co-authorship network shows shared programme authorship, not citation or collaboration history outside ICRS.
