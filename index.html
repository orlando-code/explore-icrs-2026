    Methods

    For full details see METHODOLOGY.md.

    Data sources

    Built from the official delegate list and the online programme.

    Map locations

    Institute coordinates come from Google geocoding, with manual checks and overrides where needed. If no precise address is available, the pin falls back to a state or country capital such that every attended delegate gets an approximate location.

    Travel emissions

    Return-trip CO₂e to Auckland uses the emissions.dev Travel API from each delegate's primary affiliation. Outside New Zealand (or >400 km from Auckland): economy flight. Closer NZ trips: shared car at 1.54 occupancy.

    Acting on travel emissions

    Gathering thousands of scientists for ICRS every four years builds the collaborations and knowledge-sharing with which we can "ensure a future for our coral reefs". However, we acknowledge the significant quantities of emissions required to bring together our global community in this way.

    First and foremost, we must focus on decarbonisation via direct emissions reduction. In cases where emissions are unavoidable or have already occurred, we can demonstrate our collective determination to mitigate the environmental damage through contributions towards high-quality, verified efforts to remove greenhouse gases from the atmosphere – ideally while also supporting biodiversity and healthy ecosystem function.

    Historically, such efforts have been far from perfect, and it is often difficult to identify junk credits. Despite this, high-quality projects do exist, and many universities have carefully-chosen carbon contribution schemes. For example, extensive research by the University of Cambridge's Centre for Carbon Credits means that the University requires contributions towards a REDD+ project in Sierra Leone.

    The ability of individuals to contribute will vary greatly. We encourage engaging with schemes at your university or institution where possible. And if your institution doesn't have such a scheme, why not? Engaging in such discussions could have cascading positive effects far beyond an individual purchase.

    Carbon contributions to a range of projects and prices can be made easily via sites such as Gold Standard, Cool Effect, or Greenfleet.

    As emphasised throughout ICRS: "We need to get rid of carbon now like there is no tomorrow – because, for many corals, there is no tomorrow".

    Thanks to Scott Heron and Anil Madhavapeddy for their support and valuable insights.

    Network and related talks

    Edges link people (or affiliations) who share authorship on an ICRS presentation. Non-attending co-authors appear on the graph but are not counted in attendance or emissions. Related presentations use a local-hosted large language model (LLM) to surface similar abstracts for follow-up reading and possible collaborations!

    Contacts

    Public profile links for highly connected speakers were found online and checked by hand. Emails are never bundled into the page: each request needs a Cloudflare Turnstile check and is rate-limited. Unhappy with your details here? Email rt582@cam.ac.uk and I'll remove them.

    Limitations

    • The delegate list is a pre-conference snapshot – late withdrawals still count toward emissions (though most would already have held tickets).
    • Some pins use capital-city fallbacks when no institute address is known; hundreds of routes are country-level only, which can both under- or over-estimate distance to Auckland.
    • Foreign postings (e.g. Bermuda Institute of Ocean Sciences staff based in New Caledonia) keep the institute name but map and route from the delegate's country.
    • Flights assume economy class and a single great-circle leg – real multi-leg itineraries, aircraft type, and specific effect of contrails are not modelled, so figures are indicative.
    • Estimates exclude daily local transport, accommodation, and conference operations.
    • Co-authorship is programme authorship only (talk or poster lists), not wider collaboration, and may have changed between submission and delivery.
    • Non-speaking delegates appear on the map/emissions views but not in the network unless they are also listed as co-authors.
    • Programme-only names (not on the delegate list) are retained for the network but excluded from attendance and emissions tallies.
